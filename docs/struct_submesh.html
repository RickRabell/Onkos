Detailed Description

A localized chunk of geometry that uses a single Material Instance.

Why it exists: A 3D artist might export a Car as a single file. However, the car has a metal body, rubber tires, and glass windows. A GPU draw call can only use ONE material at a time. Therefore, the engine splits the Car into three Submeshes.
